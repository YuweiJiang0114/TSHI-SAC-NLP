## Key findings (Strategy I)

1. **The decomposition is exact at the optimum.** Three independent plant optimizations at the published Fig. 3 interface, under the paper's Sec. 6 rules, give TAC 453,648.6 against the paper's coupled BARON MINLP 453,648, with the paper's structure in every plant.
2. **SAC solves the paper's best case (Strategy I) without any published design as input.** Over 3 seeds (3,000 site evaluations each), the best designs have TAC 453,923, 453,359, 453,303 (+0.06%, -0.06%, -0.08% vs the paper). They reach 99% of the paper's saving after 1,268, 1,317, 1,065 evaluations and pass the independent paper-rule audit. The final deterministic policies alone give TAC 456,928, 457,677, 455,367.
3. **Differential evolution with the same decomposition is slightly better on this single case.** Over 3 seeds it reaches TAC 452,387, 452,387, 452,396 (99% of the paper's saving after 864, 1,106, 839 evaluations); all seeds converge to the same design, with the same plant structures as SAC's and a slightly sharper interface. The decomposition, not the outer optimizer, is what makes the problem tractable.
4. Random search over the same interface space reaches only TAC 490,915.
5. **The plant subproblems are solved to within 0.05% of their global optimum.** SCIP certifies P1 and P2 exactly and P3 within one bypass charge (USD 67/yr) at every reported design.
6. **One contextual SAC policy covers all 12 coalition/role cases of Tables 3-5.** With 500 evaluations per case it matches or beats the paper on the three pair coalitions and on `--+`. It misses the two cases whose optimum leaves one plant out (`-+-`, `+-+`) and under-converges on `-++`; there, per-case DE with the same budget does better. Combining each method's best value per coalition, the Shapley allocation of Sec. 7 is recovered.
7. **Action design decides whether SAC can find the optimum.** Under the paper's 30 kW minimum duty, a fractional duty map stalls SAC at TACS ~160k (ablation below); encoding "keep at least 30 kW or eliminate the utility" in the action map removes the cliff.

## Ablation: action map without the utility-elimination snap

These two SAC runs (case "-++", seeds 1 and 2) used `configs/tshi_decomp_paper_rules.yaml`
before the action map was changed. Requests were balanced by scaling the larger side
(`balance: min`), and only 2% of each duty action saturated at the maximum duty.

Both stalled at TACS ~160.5k (paper: 176.7k) after ~1,200 evaluations, with P2 exporting
~80 kW. Under the paper's 30 kW minimum duty, any export between 80.5 and 110.5 kW
leaves a forbidden 0-30 kW cooler on P2, so the paper's optimum (full 110.5 kW export,
cooler eliminated) is isolated behind a cost cliff. The scaled balance also made P2's
export depend on P1's action. The runs were stopped (incomplete; no summary.json), and
the study was restarted with source-priority balance and a utility-elimination snap
zone. See docs/TSHI_DECOMP_SAC.md.
